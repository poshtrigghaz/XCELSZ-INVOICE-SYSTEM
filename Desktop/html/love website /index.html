My Dearest Faith

Today, the world celebrates you…
But me? I celebrate us.

Because your life is more than a date on a calendar,
It’s the beginning of every beautiful thing I’ve known.
You’re the calm after every storm,
The voice that feels like home,
The smile that makes my heart rest easy.

Sometimes I look at you and my chest gets tight...
Not from pain (lol), but from too much love sitting in one place.
You came into my life and everything just started to make sense.
You healed parts of me I didn’t even know were broken.

Faith, you’re not just my woman, You’re my answered prayer,
My peace in human form.
When I say I love you,
It’s not a line, it’s a confession.
I love you with my soul,
With the part of me that only God understands.

So today, as you celebrate your new age,
I just want you to know;
I cherish you.
I value you.
I choose you again and again.

Happy birthday, my heartbeat.
May your light never dim,
May your smile never fade,
And may you always feel how deeply you’re loved.

Forever yours,
Anozie ❤️
